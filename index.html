Nick the Bin — the 8-bit Two-Tier Britain game

Nick the Bin is a free, satirical 8-bit British beat-'em-up. You play British Man, chucking wheelie bins at custodian-helmet police officers across London — Brick Lane, 10 Downing Street, the London Eye, Tower Bridge and Westminster's Big Ben. No download; it plays in your browser on desktop and mobile.
